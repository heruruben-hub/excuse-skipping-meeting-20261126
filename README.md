200 characters or fewer, that gets you out of a pointless work meeting or Zoom call.

## The line

> My calendar auto-declined this meeting on my behalf. I have decided to trust its judgment.

90 characters. The joke is not a flimsy excuse but a surrender of the decision
itself: the calendar — the one tool every office worker already lets run their
life — declined the meeting, and the speaker simply trusts its judgment more
than their own. It is true to office life (auto-decline is a real feature, and
everyone has wished their calendar would say no for them), it takes a fresh
angle instead of the stock wifi, cat or dentist excuse, and the laugh lands in
the second sentence, where obeying a scheduling app is presented as wisdom.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line, exactly as written, to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8,
one line, no trailing newline), after validating it against the competition
gate: 1–200 characters, not only white space, single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
